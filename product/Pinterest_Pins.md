## Posting plan

- **Week 1:** Pin all 8, about 1–2 per day (not all on the same day).
- **After that:** re-pin each image to a second relevant board, about 2 weeks apart. Pinterest rewards consistent new pins more than bursts.
- **Seasonal pushes:**
  - January: Budget (06, 07) and Expenses (03): New Year and tax prep
  - February–April: Expenses & Mileage (03): tax season
  - October–December: Pricing (05): holiday sales for makers
- **Rich Pins:** Claim your Etsy shop website in Pinterest Business settings so pins show your shop info.
